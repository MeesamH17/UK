FAQ

Straight answers.

How does the publishing process work?

It begins with a consultation about your book and goals. We then recommend the editing, design, publishing, distribution and marketing support that suits you.

Do I retain ownership of my book?

Rights are set out in your contract and depend on the service you choose. We explain the terms clearly before you commit.

Can you help with editing and formatting?

Yes. Depending on your chosen service, we can support editing, interior formatting and cover design.

Can you publish paperback, hardcover and ebook editions?

We can help prepare a range of print and digital formats. Options depend on your book and chosen service.

Can you help me publish an audiobook?

Audiobook support may be available depending on your chosen service. Ask during your consultation.

Where can my book be distributed?

Depending on your service and agreement, we can help make your book available through online retailers, bookstores and distribution networks.

Do you work with first-time authors?

Yes. We work with first-time, independent and established authors, and guide you at each stage.

Can you help market an existing book?

Yes. We can discuss marketing strategies for books that are already published.

How long does the publishing process take?

Timelines depend on your manuscript, chosen services and agreement. We outline an expected schedule at the start.
